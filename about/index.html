I should probably introduce myself properly. I’m Stefan Mihajlovic, a web designer and developer from Belgrade. The photos above cover my three main settings: presentable, asleep, and supervised by a cat.

Design is one of my all-time favorite things to do. It gives all the strange little ideas in my head somewhere to go. And trust me, my head is very good at coming up with more of them.

Development became an extension of that obsession. I wanted my designs to turn into things you could actually click, use, and enjoy. That curiosity led me to the Faculty of Organizational Sciences in Belgrade, where I’m currently studying.

These days, I combine UI/UX design and frontend development to build websites, digital products, and browser extensions. I like being there for the whole thing: the first rough idea, the awkward middle bit, and that lovely moment when it all finally works.
