## Final prompt
Create a premium photographic website hero background for Font Pirate, a typography browser extension. Reference image is the brand mark ONLY: a cream lowercase a wearing a dark purple eyepatch. Reproduce this letter mark on a huge purple fabric pirate flag, without the rounded square icon tile. Wide 16:9 composition. Editorial art direction, tactile fabric and strong wind. The flag fills the right two thirds of the frame, billowing in bold elegant folds, rich violet #8054D9 and lavender highlights, the cream pirate-a emblem clearly visible on the cloth. Cropped black flagpole at far right. Background is a soft nearly black plum studio, with generous very dark empty space occupying left 45 percent for large white website headline. Dramatic directional studio light shows finely woven cloth, deep sculptural folds, a few loose threads along the edge; tangible and convincing, sophisticated fashion campaign photography. The flag flows from upper right to lower middle, slight motion in the far edge. No seascape, no landscape, no metal letters, no treasure chest, no skull, no UI, no browser, no mockup frame, no gradients as decoration, no extra lettering, no caption, no watermark. This is a full bleed photograph, not a poster layout. The only graphic on the flag is the reference pirate a mark.
