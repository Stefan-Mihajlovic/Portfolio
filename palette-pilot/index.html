Questions, answered

Good to know.

Still curious? Ask me anything.

What is included for free?

Unlimited color picking, HEX, RGB, HSL and OKLCH values, quick copy, six recent colors, one saved palette, basic contrast checking, and TXT/CSS exports.

What does Pro unlock?

Unlimited named palettes and folders, page and image extraction, palette generation, the full accessibility studio, color-blindness simulation, semantic tokens, side-panel mode, keyboard shortcuts, advanced exports, and local backup/import.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Palette Pilot Pro is a one-time $9.99 payment with lifetime access and future Pro updates.

Where is my palette data stored?

Your palette data stays in local extension storage. Image analysis runs locally, and page extraction only runs when you invoke it.

Can I use Pro on more than one installation?

Yes. One Pro license supports up to three active installations, with local backup and import for moving your workspace.
