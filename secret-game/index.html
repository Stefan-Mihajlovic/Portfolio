Flight manual

The first flagship arrives at 500 points. The next arrives at 1,000, then one every 1,000 points. Break all three armor segments to bring it down. Each breach changes its fire pattern. Watch the edge markers for incoming squadrons. Break a segment to clear enemy fire. Destroy two ships in a Bastion convoy to expose its core for double damage. Hold Shift for fine steering, or make short drags on touch. Skim a projectile for five points.
