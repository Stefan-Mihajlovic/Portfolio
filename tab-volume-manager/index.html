Before you turn it up.

Need a hand? Get in touch.

What is included for free?

Per-tab volume up to 500%, mute and reset, Bass Boost, Voice Clarity, adjustable effect intensity, a six-band equalizer, one custom preset, per-site settings, and light/dark themes.

What does every Pro plan include?

Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime unlock Smart Limiter, Adaptive Volume, Movie Dialogue, the full 10-band equalizer, Multi-tab Mixer, Sleep Timer, and the complete Pro suite.

How are the plans different?

Monthly and Yearly include four custom presets. Lifetime is a one-time payment and adds unlimited presets plus volume up to 1500%.

Where are settings and presets stored?

Your settings and presets stay in the extension’s local storage on your device, rather than in an online account.

Does it process audio all the time?

No. Tab audio is captured and processed only while Tab Volume Manager is active. You stay in control of when it runs.
